# FungInfer
Scripts and examples of datasets for the inference of the branching characteristics of a fungal network

---
## Inference Based On Math Model
**simulation.ipynb** simulates the process, computes and plots the empirical tail distribution in lengths of open segments and infers the branching rates from the simulated data.

**plot.ipynb** plots the graph of the mycelium and the empirical tail distribution in lengths of open segments for the pooled experimental data.

**inference.ipynb** infers the relative branching rates from data pooled from all the growth experiments in standard condition and computes the standard deviation using bootstrap.

**comparison.ipynb** plots the empirical tail distributions in lengths of open and of closed segments for both experimental and simulated data and compares the experimental, simulated and theoretical distributions.
